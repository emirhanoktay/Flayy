## Features

- **Star-based rewards** — Customers earn stars based on the purchases they make.
- **Reward mechanism** — Collected stars turn into rewards (e.g. 10 stars = 1 coffee, 15 stars = 1 dessert).
- **QR-powered transactions** — Earning stars and redeeming rewards happen through QR codes.
- **Three roles, one app** — Separate logins and dashboards for customers, cashiers, and café owners.
- **Campaign management** — Businesses define and manage their own rewards and campaigns.

## How It Works

The customer logs into the app and sees their personal QR code. During checkout, the cashier scans this QR and stars are added to the customer based on their purchase. Once the stars reach the defined thresholds, they turn into rewards (for example, a free coffee at 10 stars). The café owner manages campaigns and rewards from their own dashboard, while the cashier handles day-to-day transactions. All three roles live in the same app but operate through separate logins.
